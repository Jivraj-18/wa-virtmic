# Kestrel K2 price list, 2027

Fictional test data for experiment E2. Tindleworth Robotics and Kestrel K2 are invented. All prices in US dollars, excluding tax.

## General terms
Prices apply to orders confirmed between 1 January and 31 December 2027. Subscriptions are billed yearly in advance.
Drones stay the property of Tindleworth Robotics on the subscription plans; on the Purchase plan they belong to the customer.
Shipping inside the pilot cities is free. Training for up to four operators is included in every plan.
